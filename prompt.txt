### Option 2: The "Keyword Combination" Prompt
Use this if your keywords are slightly different and you need help connecting them into a coherent story.

**Prompt:**
> "I am creating a YouTube video. Here is a list of keywords related to the content: **[List keywords, e.g., 'sourdough starter, feeding schedule, discard recipes, bubbling not rising']** .
>
> Please generate a compelling YouTube caption and a set of hashtags based on this list.
> - **The Caption:** Should read like a human wrote it, connecting these keywords into a helpful tip or a question for the viewer.
> - **The Hashtags:** Should include specific tags derived from the list (like #SourdoughDiscard) and broader category tags (like #Baking)."

---

### Option 3: The "Hashtag-Only" Prompt
Use this if you are happy with your current caption/title but just need a powerful set of hashtags based on your keyword research.

**Prompt:**
> "Generate a list of 20 highly effective YouTube hashtags based on this core list of keywords: **[Insert keywords]** .
>
> **Rules for the hashtags:**
> - Include a mix of **broad** (popular) and **specific** (niche) tags.
> - Do not use any hashtags that are irrelevant or spammy.
> - Prioritize hashtags that people are actually searching for based on the keywords provided.
> - Group them by relevance, with the top 5 being the most important."

---
